About LinguaNova

Because communication changes lives.

LinguaNova was founded on the belief that language is the foundation of education, culture, opportunity, knowledge, and human connection—and that artificial intelligence creates the greatest value when guided by human expertise, ethics, and cultural understanding.

Our story

Bridging language, knowledge, and technology.

LinguaNova is a Canadian language innovation company combining professional language services, AI solutions, educational innovation, research consulting, digital publishing, and community-centered initiatives into one ecosystem.

Its long-term aspiration is to become a recognized leader in language innovation while contributing meaningfully to education, research, and preservation of linguistic diversity.

“A company should exist for a reason greater than making a profit.”

Founder

Dr. Fredy Mendieta Rodriguez

Dr. Fredy Mendieta Rodriguez is the Founder of LinguaNova Group, bringing extensive expertise in linguistics, educational research, and language acquisition. Holding a doctoral degree and bilingual proficiency in English and Spanish, his work focuses on andragogy, popular education, and promoting social justice in academic instruction.

His professional background includes leading community outreach for educational inclusion and women's literacy initiatives with Universidad Nacional de San Luis and FLACSO, as well as mentoring at the LOBO Language Acquisition Lab. Through LinguaNova, Dr. Mendieta Rodriguez bridges rigorous academic research with practical, human-centered language and technology solutions.
