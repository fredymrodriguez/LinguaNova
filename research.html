Research & Academic Solutions

Rigorous support across the research lifecycle.

LinguaNova’s research work is designed for researchers, institutions, organizations, and professionals seeking evidence-based, methodologically informed solutions.

Research Consulting

Study design, methodology, data interpretation, and dissemination support.

Literature Reviews

Evidence synthesis using systematic and transparent approaches appropriate to the project.

Grant Development

Funding strategy, research planning, and proposal-development consulting.

Program Evaluation

Qualitative, quantitative, or mixed-method evaluation for educational, governmental, nonprofit, and organizational initiatives.

Consulting to improve scholarly writing while preserving authorship and academic integrity.

Linguistic Analysis

Specialized work in phonetics, phonology, syntax, sociolinguistics, discourse analysis, bilingualism, and second-language acquisition.
