Privacy Policy

How LinguaNova Group collects, uses, and protects your information.

Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when requesting a consultation, including your name, email address, organization, and project details. We also use third-party scheduling tools (such as Calendly) which may collect data necessary to facilitate your booking.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to respond to your inquiries, schedule consultations, and evaluate how our language, education, research, and AI services can support your goals. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Data Security and Confidentiality

We take the protection of your data seriously. Please do not submit highly sensitive, medical, or confidential documents through our initial public scheduling forms. Secure document exchange protocols are established during formal consultations.
