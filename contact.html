FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is LinguaNova only a translation company?

No. The Master Plan defines LinguaNova as a language innovation and knowledge company integrating language services, AI, education, research, publishing, and community initiatives.

Does LinguaNova use AI instead of professionals?

No. The company’s stated model is human-centered: AI should enhance expertise and reduce repetitive work while preserving human judgment and professional responsibility.

Can one project involve several divisions?

Yes. Integrated solutions are a core differentiator. Projects may combine language, education, research, AI, and publishing capabilities as needed.

Can I upload sensitive documents through this prototype?

No. Secure document exchange is a future website capability in the Master Plan and has not been implemented in this static prototype.
